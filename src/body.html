Draft steps

These are placeholders while the instructions are being written. Measurements and order may change before the workshop.

  1. Press and fuse

    Press the canvas and lining flat. If you’re using interfacing, center it on the wrong side of each outer panel and fuse it with a pressing cloth.

    Iron: cotton, steam
    Photo to come: interfacing centered on the outer panel

  2. Cover the zipper ends

    Fold each tab in half and press, then fold the raw edges in to the crease. Slip one over each end of the zipper and stitch across close to the fold. Trim the zipper so the tabbed length is 9 in.

    Stitch 3.0 mm⅛ in from fold

    Slide the pull to the middle before you trim, or it can run off the cut end.

  3. Sew the first side of the zipper

    Stack from the bottom: outer panel right side up, zipper face down along the top edge, lining right side down. Clip the three layers together and sew along the zipper tape.

    Zipper footStitch 2.5 mm¼ in from edge
    Photo to come: the three-layer stack, clipped

  4. Sew the second side

    Repeat with the other outer and lining panel on the free zipper tape. Line the panels up with the first pair so the sides match.

    Zipper footStitch 2.5 mm

  5. Topstitch along the zipper

    Press the fabric away from the teeth on both sides. Topstitch through the outer and lining together, close to the fold, so the lining can’t catch in the zipper.

    Stitch 3.0 mm⅛ in from fold

  6. Open the zipper halfway

    Do this before you sew around the edges.

    If the zipper is closed when you sew the perimeter, there’s no way to turn the pouch right side out.

  7. Sew around the edges

    Open the pieces out so the two outers face each other and the two linings face each other, with the zipper teeth pointing toward the lining. Clip all around and sew, leaving a gap in the bottom of the lining.

    ½ in seamLeave a 3 in gap
    Photo to come: outers and linings clipped, gap marked

  8. Box the corners (optional)

    For a pouch that stands up, pinch each bottom corner flat so the side seam sits on the bottom seam. Mark a line 1 in from the point, sew across it, and trim.

    1 in acrossTrim to ¼ in

  9. Turn, close and press

    Turn the pouch through the gap and push the corners out. Sew the gap closed close to the edge, tuck the lining inside, and press.

If something goes wrong

Common fixes

My needle keeps breaking

Switch to a 100/16 denim needle and slow right down near the zipper tabs. Never sew over the metal zipper stops.

The machine skips stitches at thick corners

Fold a scrap of canvas to the same height and put it behind the presser foot so the foot stays level as it climbs the seam.

The lining bunches up by the zipper

Press both sides away from the teeth before topstitching, and check the lining is lying flat underneath before each side.

The corners look rounded after turning

Clip the seam allowance at each corner before turning, then push the corners out with a point turner or a chopstick.
